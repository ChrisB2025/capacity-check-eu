Which industries would a renovation programme press on?

The same public spending shock, a share of GDP spent on housing and energy renovation in one year, is applied in each EU member state. It is traced through Eurostat's inter-country input-output tables to jobs by industry, then compared with what firms report about labour shortages. The shock is an identical financial shock, not an identical physical programme.
