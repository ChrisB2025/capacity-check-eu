Sweden: which occupations?

A demonstration, not a validation. No Swedish programme with documented bottlenecks exists to test this against. The extra Swedish jobs from the run are split into four-digit occupations using the occupation mix of each of Statistics Sweden's 16 industry groups (YREG54BAS, employees only, so the self-employed are missing). Architecture and engineering shares the mix of all business services, which is why cleaners and warehouse staff appear.
