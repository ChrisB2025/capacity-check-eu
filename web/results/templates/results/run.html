Share of each programme's jobs that fall on tight or binding lines

Jobs created anywhere in the EU by each member state's programme. Hover or tap a country; select it for its industry detail.

How to read it

  • Requirement comes first: what the shock needs, by industry and where the output is produced.
  • Availability second: how often firms in each industry report labour as limiting, against that series' own history.
  • The rating combines the two. Tight: the shock adds at least {{ p.thresholds.pressure_low|pct:0 }} to the industry's employment, or shortage reports are above their median. Binding: the shock adds more than {{ p.thresholds.pressure_high|pct:0 }} and shortage reports are in the top quarter of their history. Where evidence is missing the line gets no rating, never a forced score.
  • The headline separates member states only weakly: construction is under pressure everywhere, and shortage reports are high in most industries in 2025 and 2026.
