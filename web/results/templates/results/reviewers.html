For reviewers

Thank you for looking at this. Capacity Check Europe is a low-cost demonstrator from Modern Money Lab Europe's Real Resource Scoring project. It asks one question: if every EU member state spent the same share of its GDP on renovating homes, which industries would that spending press on, and are those industries already short of labour?

It traces a spending shock through Eurostat's input-output tables to 64 industries in each member state, turns the output needed into jobs, and sets that against how often firms report labour shortages. Each industry in each country gets an estimated risk category: clear, tight or binding. These are risk indicators for early screening, not forecasts and not verdicts on whether a programme can be delivered.

All results are provisional: the spending mix has not yet been confirmed after review, and the review you are doing is part of that.
