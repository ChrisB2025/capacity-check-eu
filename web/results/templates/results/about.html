Method in brief

  1. Direct demand. A composition table splits the spending across the products bought directly.
  2. Supply chains. Eurostat's FIGARO inter-country input-output tables ({{ industries }} industries, 50 economies) give the output required from each industry in each economy, split into home, rest of EU and outside EU.
  3. Jobs. Persons employed per euro of output, from Eurostat's national accounts.
  4. Pressure. Extra persons as a share of the industry's employment.
  5. Availability. The share of firms naming labour as limiting (DG ECFIN business surveys), placed in its own history.
  6. Rating. Clear, tight, binding or no rating, from pressure and availability together. Thresholds are parameters.
  7. Headline. The share of a programme's jobs on tight or binding lines.
  8. Sweden. Swedish jobs split into occupations and compared with official forecasts.
  9. Robustness. Other compositions, thresholds, slack indicator and FIGARO edition.
  10. Plausibility. France and Ireland against official job estimates.

The full method note, composition table, data register and decisions log are in the repository: {{ REPO_URL }}/tree/main/docs.
